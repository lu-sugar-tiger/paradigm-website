Questions? Answers.

What can our team personalize?

Choose from 7 Road colors and 3 designs, then complete the kit with player names and numbers. Paradigm applies your team identity within the selected design.

Do the jersey and shorts reverse together?

Yes. Both pieces reverse together between your selected Road side and the white Home side.

Can we supply a different design?

No. Each design is crafted by Paradigm to keep the visual language consistent across the complete kit.

How is the artwork applied?

The kit is dye-sublimated. Heat transfers the artwork into the polyester fibers, leaving a smooth surface without a raised film layer.

How long does delivery take?

Delivery is typically within four weeks after the design, roster, sizing, and quantity are confirmed.
