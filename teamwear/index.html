Teamwear FAQ.

What can our team personalize?

Choose Essential, Classic, or Signature and one of seven Road colors in the builder. Team name, logo, typography, player names, numbers, roster, sizing, quantity, price, and production timing are confirmed after inquiry.

Do the jersey and shorts reverse together?

Yes. Both pieces reverse between the white Home side and your selected Road color.

Can we supply a different pattern?

Basketball 01 uses Essential, Classic, or Signature as its design foundation. This keeps the proportions consistent across the complete kit.

How is the artwork applied?

The kit is dye sublimated. Heat fixes the artwork into the polyester, leaving a smooth surface without a raised film layer.

How long does delivery take?

Delivery is typically within one month, about four weeks, after the design, roster, sizing, and quantity are confirmed.
